Small documentation improvements and reports of confusing steps are useful.

## Propose a bounded change

- Explain the problem, intended behavior, scope and how the change will be checked.
- For features, policy changes or wider restructuring, agree on a plan with a maintainer first.
- Keep each change focused and report the commands you ran with their actual results.
- Disclose material AI assistance. Do not upload private prompts or transcripts.
- Never include secrets, personal data or proprietary material.

Contributions use the project's MIT license. A maintainer must explicitly accept the reviewed revision.
